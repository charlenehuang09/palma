Palma Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Palma ("we," "our," or "the app") is a communication app designed to help users express needs, feelings, and thoughts through simple communication cards and spoken messages.

Information We Collect

Palma does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use the app.

Palma does not directly collect or store personal information, communication history, or other user information on our own servers.

Palma does not use analytics or crash-reporting services to collect information about users or their use of the app.

AI Processing

When you use Palma's sentence-generation feature, the communication cards and text you select are sent to Google's Gemini service to generate a natural-language sentence.

This information is sent only for the purpose of providing the sentence generation feature. Palma does not maintain a database of these requests or store the generated sentences on our own servers.

Information sent to Gemini is subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies. For more information about how Google handles information submitted to its AI services, please refer to Google's privacy documentation.

Information Sharing

Palma does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. The only external service used to provide the AI sentence-generation feature is Google's Gemini service, as described above.

Data Security

Because Palma does not maintain user accounts or store personal information on our own servers, Palma does not maintain a database of user information. Information transmitted to Gemini is handled by Google's services and is subject to Google's applicable security and privacy practices.

Children's Privacy

Palma is designed as a communication tool that may be used by children and families. Palma does not require users to create accounts or provide personal information.

Users should avoid entering sensitive personal information into communication cards or text that is sent to Gemini.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Palma's privacy practices, please contact us through the Palma Support page.
